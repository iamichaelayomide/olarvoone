Frequently asked questions

Can I use Olarvo One offline?

Yes. Everything runs locally on your device. Sales, products, and receipts update immediately without an active internet connection.

Do my customers need an app to receive receipts?

No. They receive a standard message with a PDF receipt or invoice on WhatsApp or SMS. There is nothing for them to install.

Can I export my data anytime?

Anytime. Export sales, products, customers, and financial summaries as CSV from Settings with a single click.

Is my business data private?

100% private. We do not sell merchant data or run advertising trackers. Your numbers stay strictly yours.
