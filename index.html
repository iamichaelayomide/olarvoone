Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Olarvo One.

How does Olarvo One work when my internet is disconnected?

Olarvo One uses browser storage (LocalStorage & IndexedDB) to save every sale, product update, and invoice locally on your phone or laptop. You can record sales and print receipts without any active internet connection.

Can I send receipts to my customers on WhatsApp?

Yes! When you complete any sale or create an invoice, you can click "WhatsApp Receipt" to instantly open WhatsApp with an itemized, formatted digital receipt ready to send to your customer.

Can I switch currencies if I operate outside Nigeria?

Yes. In the Settings tab, you can switch between Nigerian Naira (₦), Kenyan Shilling (KSh), Ghanaian Cedi (GH₵), South African Rand (R), US Dollars ($), and Euros (€).

Do you share or sell my store data?

Never. Olarvo One does not run advertising trackers. Your sales numbers, inventory levels, and customer lists remain confidential and under your sole control.
