Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

Offline-First & Zero Tracker Commitment: Olarvo One is architected with a strict privacy-by-design approach. We do not embed behavioral advertising trackers or sell merchant business transactions to third-party brokers.

1. Local Device Data Storage

Olarvo One utilizes local web storage technologies (including browser LocalStorage and IndexedDB) on your device. All product lists, sales logs, client contacts, and store financial configurations are stored directly in your local environment.

2. Offline Operations & Network Telemetry

You can utilize all core POS, inventory adjustment, receipt generation, and AI insight modules completely disconnected from the internet. When you are offline, no outbound network packets are dispatched.

3. Customer Information & WhatsApp Sharing

When you generate digital receipts or invoices and elect to share them via WhatsApp, the recipient's phone number and itemized receipt details are passed directly to your local WhatsApp application client. No intermediate ad server intercepts or harvests this communication.

4. Data Ownership & Deletion

You maintain full ownership of your data at all times. You can export your data to CSV or reset your local store data at any time via the Settings panel.
